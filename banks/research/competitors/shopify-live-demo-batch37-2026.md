# Shopify live-demo research — Batch 37: Impulse:Apothecary

**Observed:** 2026-10-26  
**Official listing:** https://themes.shopify.com/themes/impulse/presets/apothecary  
**Rendered storefront:** https://impulse-theme-jewelry.myshopify.com/

## Scope

Inspected home, All collection, Amber Candle PDP and empty cart through the rendered browser accessibility tree. Read-only only: no shopper actions, screenshot, viewport/mobile, Theme Editor, checkout or performance testing.

## Observations

- **Home:** scent-led hero with product hotspots, brand propositions, featured oils, limited-edition promo, Tonka shelf, scent-family browse, quality/returns and sustainability/newsletter modules.
- **Collection:** 110 products; Filter entry; alphabetical default sort with several choices; sale labels and four pages. No controls activated.
- **Product:** Amber Candle shows $68, scent narrative, approx. 50-hour burn copy, purchase controls, usage/FAQ/shipping panels and related products. Page states this demo's products cannot be purchased; some candle copy refers to applying to skin.
- **Cart:** empty with Continue shopping, Recently Viewed and Popular Picks. Picks use generic “Product title” labels.

Ingredient, efficacy, sustainability, certification, product, price, availability and service claims remain unverified.

## Coverage

- Selected homes: **67/127** (51 indexed HTML + 16 browser-rendered); 60 remain.
- Rendered pages: **64** across 16 selected presets.
- Indexed-document counts remain separate. Interaction, mobile, editor, checkout and Lighthouse counts remain zero.
