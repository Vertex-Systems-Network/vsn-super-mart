# Shopify live demo public HTML — Batch 2 (2026-10-25)

A further **4 actual Shopify demo stores** were inspected by fetching their public homepage, collection, PDP and, when accessible, empty-cart HTML. In combination with [batch 1](shopify-live-demo-report-2026.md), this brings the Research Bank to **8 Shopify stores / 28 page documents**. Six demos correspond to selected popular-theme preset records; Area Technical and Flow from batch 1 remain useful extra references.

**Evidence:** Empire Depot public HTML exposes quick shop and compare controls, multiple stock/pre-order states, professional taxonomy and promotion shelves; Wonder has product subscription claims, ingredient details and complementary pairing; Be Yours shows skincare set construction, inventory and testimonials; Taste free theme has sorting/filter, beverage catalog and honest empty cart with shipping calculated at checkout.

**Not tested:** Whether checkout/add-to-cart/compare/set-builder actions work, actual mobile layout/gesture, screenshot pixels, Lighthouse, or private theme editor settings. Empty cart is NOT a non-empty cart transaction. Vendor claims, reviews and demo copy are not validations.
