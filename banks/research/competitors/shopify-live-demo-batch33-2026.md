# Shopify live-demo research — Batch 33: Pipeline

**Observed:** 2026-10-26  
**Official listing:** https://themes.shopify.com/themes/pipeline/presets/pipeline  
**Rendered storefront:** https://pipeline-theme-fashion.myshopify.com/

## Scope

Inspected home, All collection, Callie Layered Looker Cardigan PDP and empty cart through the rendered browser accessibility tree. Read-only evidence; no shopper actions, screenshot, viewport/mobile, Theme Editor, checkout or performance testing.

## Observations

- **Home:** fashion hero, fabric product tabs, curated seasonal collections/lookbooks, best sellers, Shop the Look hotspots, top collections, newsletter and service panels.
- **Collection:** Product Type, Color, Size, Fabric, Fit, Price and Availability facets, sort control, 2/3/4-column choices and six pages. Result count was not exposed. No controls activated.
- **Product:** eight gallery entries, $448 price, beige/white color and alternate charcoal link, size guide, quantity stepper, low-stock copy, add-to-cart/buy-now, care/shipping accordions, cross-sell and recommendations.
- **Cart:** empty state, Continue Browsing, spend-more-for-free-shipping line and delivery FAQ links.

Treat stock, materials, sustainability, promotions, prices and fulfillment claims as unverified sample copy. Collection page-count and rendered affordances do not establish interactions.

## Coverage

- Selected homes: **63/127** (51 indexed HTML + 12 browser-rendered); 64 remain.
- Rendered pages: **48** across 12 selected presets.
- Indexed-document counts remain separate. Interaction, mobile, editor, checkout and Lighthouse counts remain zero.
