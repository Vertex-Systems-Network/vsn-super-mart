# Shopify live-demo research — Batch 31: Be Yours:Sheen

**Observed:** 2026-10-26  
**Official preset listing:** https://themes.shopify.com/themes/be-yours/presets/sheen  
**Rendered storefront:** https://beyours-theme-clothing.myshopify.com/

## Scope and evidence

Opened the official Shopify preset listing and its View demo. Inspected the home page, Clothing collection, Shimmer Dress product page and empty cart using the rendered browser accessibility tree. This is read-only rendered-page evidence. No shopper action, screenshot, viewport measurement, mobile render, Theme Editor, checkout or performance test was performed.

## Page observations

- **Home:** sale announcement and campaign hero; NEW IN/CLOTHING/BRANDS/JOURNAL/EXPLORE navigation; seasonal collection tabs, category tiles, Shop the Look, sale modules, testimonials, Instagram gallery and newsletter popup. A featured Shimmer Dress product module appears lower on the page.
- **Collection:** Clothing collection reports 59 products. Visible facets cover color, size, price and brand; cards show swatches, prices/sale prices, ratings, choose-options and quick-view affordances. No filter, sort or pagination action was performed.
- **Product:** Shimmer Dress page shows six gallery media entries, €98 regular/€49 sale price, countdown, color and size options, low-stock copy, size-help popup, add-to-cart/buy-now controls, pickup availability, sticky cart content, details and fit/shipping FAQ entries. No controls were activated.
- **Cart:** empty-state message with Return to Shop link, alongside home delivery, pickup and collection-point copy. No product was added and checkout was not opened.

## Design implications and claim handling

Pair campaign storytelling with coherent category destinations and keep fit guidance beside size selection. Preserve a direct product-discovery route from the empty cart. Discount, countdown, inventory, review, shipping, pickup and returns statements are visible sample copy, not validated merchant claims.
