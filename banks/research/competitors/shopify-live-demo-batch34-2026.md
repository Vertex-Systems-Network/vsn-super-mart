**Observed:** 2026-10-26  
**Official listing:** https://themes.shopify.com/themes/focal/presets/focal  
**Rendered storefront:** https://focal-theme-carbon.myshopify.com/

## Scope

Inspected home, iPhone Cases collection, Leather Case for AirPods Pro PDP and empty cart through the rendered browser accessibility tree. Read-only only: no shopper actions, screenshot, viewport/mobile, Theme Editor, checkout or performance testing.

## Observations

- **Home:** device/category navigation, Heritage Collection hero, best-seller/new/bundle tabs with color variants, design-process editorial, product hotspots, blogs, collaboration pages, featured PDP, press, office story and service/newsletter modules.
- **Collection:** iPhone Cases shows **39 products**, device-generation links, Color/Price/Product type/Availability facets, best-selling sort and pagination. No controls activated.
- **Product:** Leather Case for AirPods Pro shows gallery/zoom affordances, $39.99 sale price, SKU, Black/Brown/Sage choices, quantity, stock copy, add-to-cart/buy-now, tech details, warranty/shipping/payment accordions, Buy It With and related recommendations.
- **Cart:** empty state with Start Shopping link; footer includes merchant shipping/service/payment statements.

Device compatibility, stock, discounts, reviews, material and fulfillment claims are sample content and unverified.
