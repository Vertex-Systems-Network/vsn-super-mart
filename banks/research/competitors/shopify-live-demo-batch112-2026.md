PDP names NAP as the supplier; not validated.
- **Cart:** [Empty cart](https://wonder-theme-interior.myshopify.com/cart) with continue shopping, login prompt, category/service links and newsletter.

## Design note

High-consideration furniture shopping benefits from room taxonomy, material and feature facets, dimensions, configuration and service details. Verify warranty/delivery claims before relying on them.
