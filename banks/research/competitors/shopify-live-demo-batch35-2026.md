# Shopify live-demo research — Batch 35: Local:Thrive

**Observed:** 2026-10-26  
**Official listing:** https://themes.shopify.com/themes/local/presets/thrive  
**Rendered storefront:** https://local-theme-thrive.myshopify.com/

## Scope

Inspected home, All collection, Gut Health Ground Coffee PDP and empty cart through the rendered browser accessibility tree. Read-only only: no shopper actions, screenshot, viewport/mobile, Theme Editor, checkout or performance testing.

## Observations

- **Home:** coupon announcement, category/product tabs, mushroom coffee hero, benefit-led category tiles, ingredient/science editorial, Shop the Look products, testimonials, FAQ, guarantee and newsletter.
- **Collection:** 14 products; category shortcuts; Benefits, Availability and Price facets; alphabetical default sort with several sort options. No controls activated.
- **Product:** Gut Health Ground Coffee shows sale/regular pricing, variant links, quantity and purchase controls; pickup locations, collapsible details, complementary product, benefit copy and ingredient narrative.
- **Cart:** empty with Continue browsing.

Product, health/efficacy, ingredient, reviews, discount, availability and guarantee copy are merchant claims and remain unverified.

## Coverage

- Selected homes: **65/127** (51 indexed HTML + 14 browser-rendered); 62 remain.
- Rendered pages: **56** across 14 selected presets.
- Indexed-document counts remain separate. Interaction, mobile, editor, checkout and Lighthouse counts remain zero.
