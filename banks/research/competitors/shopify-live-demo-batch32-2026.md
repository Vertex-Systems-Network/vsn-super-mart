# Shopify live-demo research — Batch 32: Palo Alto

**Observed:** 2026-10-26  
**Official preset listing:** https://themes.shopify.com/themes/palo-alto/presets/palo-alto  
**Rendered storefront:** https://palo-alto-theme-main.myshopify.com/

## Scope and evidence

Opened the official Shopify preset listing and View demo. Inspected home, All Clothing collection, Sylvia Blazer PDP and empty cart using the rendered browser accessibility tree. Read-only rendered-page evidence; no shopper actions, screenshot, viewport measurement, mobile, Theme Editor, checkout or performance test.

## Page observations

- **Home:** campaign hero, countdown promotion, category mosaic, collection promotion row, featured collection, lookbook/editorial, reviews, social modules, Shop the Look product hotspots with quick-buy affordances, press, service FAQs and newsletter.
- **Collection:** All Clothing shows **304 products**, category shortcuts, availability/color/size/product-type/price filters and 13-page pagination. Product cards show badges, price comparisons, color counts and quick-buy controls. No filter/sort/page action performed.
- **Product:** Sylvia Blazer PDP has seven gallery media entries, $220 price, stock status, XS–XL sizes, three colors, quantity stepper, add-to-cart/buy-now, limited-time countdown, fit guidance, shipping/returns, Buy It With, reviews and recommendation tabs. No controls activated.
- **Cart:** empty-state message with links to New Arrivals, Staff Picks, Bestselling, Sale and Shop All; free-shipping copy visible. Checkout not opened.

## Design implications and claim handling

Pair campaign modules with clear catalog destinations and put fit guidance near size selection. The empty cart offers multiple routes back to discovery. Demo stock, discount, review, sustainability, material and fulfillment claims remain unverified.
