## Research interpretation

Across the Tier 4 cohort, interactive patterns included gallery zoom, variant options, product-information disclosures/tabs, storefront menus and locale selectors. These observations support interaction-pattern comparisons; they do not verify private Theme Editor settings, transaction completion, accessibility conformance, performance, or merchant claims.
