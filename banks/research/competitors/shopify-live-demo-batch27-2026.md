# Shopify live demo audit — Batch 27 (Vision)

Observed: 2026-10-26  
Selected preset: `vision:vision`  
Official listing: https://themes.shopify.com/themes/vision/presets/vision  
Demo: https://vision-main.myshopify.com/

The official listing's View demo opened Vision - Main. Read-only rendered accessibility trees were inspected for the homepage, All Products collection, Indoor WiFi Smart Camera PDP and cart.

- **Home:** Three-slide smart-home hero, promotions and best-seller shelf with category tabs, variants, ratings, stock and pre-order states; education, category, brand, editorial, customer quote and newsletter modules.
- **Collection:** 20 products, brand/availability/price/color facets, sorting and compare controls. Cards display compare boxes, variants, ratings, stock and badges.
- **Product:** Smart camera PDP links gallery, price, ratings and color/material variants to stock statement, product specs, policy panels, discount code, paired and related items.
- **Cart:** Empty state offers Continue shopping and recently viewed products.

No filter, sort, compare, variant, add-to-cart or checkout actions were executed. No screenshot, viewport, mobile, editor, keyboard or performance tests were captured. Demo itself says goods are unavailable for purchase; merchant claims remain unverified.

Coverage is 57/127 distinct homes (51 indexed HTML + 6 selected browser-rendered) and 24 rendered pages across six selected presets. Next: Ignite:Ignite.
