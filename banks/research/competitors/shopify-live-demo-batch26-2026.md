# Shopify live demo audit — Batch 26 (Impulse Dune)

Observed: 2026-10-26  
Selected listing: https://themes.shopify.com/themes/impulse/presets/dune  
Demo host opened by View demo: https://impulse-theme-apparel.myshopify.com/

The official Dune listing preview opened an Impulse Theme — Apparel storefront. The site links a Dune-specific Theme Features page, but the storefront branding is generic, so the mapping caveat is retained. Read-only rendered accessibility trees were inspected for home, All collection, a 5 Panel PDP and cart.

## Observations

- **Home:** Comfort-led hero, new-arrival quick-view shelf, Shop the Look, promotional tiles, category links, retail-location module, product shelves, timed promotion and newsletter.
- **Collection:** All reports 249 products with filtering, sorting and 12-page pagination. Product cards expose quick view; seasonal promotions follow the grid.
- **Product:** 5 Panel PDP has S–XL sizes, size-chart link, price, shipping/stock statements, add/buy actions, product details, trust copy, complementary picks and recommendations.
- **Cart:** Empty state provides Continue shopping, recently viewed and popular product shelves.

## Evidence limits

No filters, sort, quick-view, variant, add-to-cart or checkout actions were executed. No screenshot, viewport, mobile, Theme Editor, keyboard or performance check. Merchant stock, shipping, price, sustainability and promotional statements are unverified sample copy.
