## Synthesis

The first-party free themes expose different interaction patterns while keeping product context visible: Dawn uses a search dialog, Horizon and Savor expose selectable product options, Trade expands product features in place, and Tinker/Atelier offer reversible image zoom views. The sample is limited to one targeted control per preset; it does not cover every template, keyboard flow, Theme Editor, checkout or performance.
