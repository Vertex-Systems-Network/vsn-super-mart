## Observations

- Home presents a satin-separates campaign hero, sale messaging, brand philosophy, category entry points, countdown promotion, a Statement Tops shelf with color/size cues, lookbook hotspots, video content, featured edits, testimonials, brand story, journal and newsletter.
- Tops shows 34 results, alphabetical sort, and Availability, Category, Color, Size and Price filters. Nothing was activated.
- Alex Metallic Top shows $229, three colors, five sizes, size guide, stock copy, quantity and Add to Bag controls, shipping/material/fit copy and product accordions. No purchase or product control was used.
- Cart says it is empty and exposes Continue Shopping, service/returns/shipping copy, demo attribution and collection links.
- The demo states product content and imagery were supplied by Boskemper for demonstration.

## Mapping caveat

After View demo on the official Stiletto listing, the listing iframe src exposed a Vogue host, while the loaded STO Stiletto page title and its visible internal links (including the homepage and cart links) resolved to `stiletto-theme-stiletto.myshopify.com`. The Stiletto destination pages were opened from those visible storefront links and verified. The host discrepancy is retained for follow-up.
