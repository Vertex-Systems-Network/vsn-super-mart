# Shopify rendered demo research — Batch 110: Sleek:Wildpeak

**Date:** 2026-10-27  
**Official listing:** https://themes.shopify.com/themes/sleek/presets/wildpeak  
**Verified demo:** https://sleek-wildpeak.myshopify.com/

## Read-only page observations

- **Home:** Shipping banner, multi-slide campaign hero, product tabs, category/editorial tiles, product/review carousel, promotion tiles, bundle section, service benefits, flash-sale content, shop-the-look, testimonials, FAQs and footer. Copy and product imagery mix outdoor apparel with unrelated fashion content.
- **Collection:** [Best Sellers](https://sleek-wildpeak.myshopify.com/collections/best-sellers) shows 20 products and facets for product type, price, color, size and availability. Product types include accessories, cashmeres, dresses, jackets/coats, pants, skirts and T-shirts. Best selling sort and page 2 link are visible; controls were not used.
- **Product:** [Emma 3L Shell Jacket Green](https://sleek-wildpeak.myshopify.com/products/emma-3l-shell-jacket-green) shows $669 price, gallery, color and S–XL selectors, size-chart table, inventory text, quantity/add-to-cart controls, shipping copy and accordions. Review quotes and lower editorial modules refer to other products or unrelated beachwear, furniture and linen content.
- **Cart:** [Empty cart](https://sleek-wildpeak.myshopify.com/cart) suggests Pants, Shorts, Jackets and Shoes collections; newsletter and footer remain visible.

## Design note

The catalog facet model is broad for apparel. Keep the taxonomy and PDP recommendations coherent with the product category: unrelated reviews and editorial modules weaken trust. Seller, inventory, shipping and product claims have not been independently verified.

- **320 counted rendered pages across 81 presets** in the repository source-of-truth counters (four pages added in this batch).
