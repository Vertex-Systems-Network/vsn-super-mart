# Shopify live-demo research — Batch 36: Impulse:Terrain

**Observed:** 2026-10-26  
**Official listing:** https://themes.shopify.com/themes/impulse/presets/terrain  
**Rendered storefront:** https://impulse-theme-sports.myshopify.com/

## Scope

Inspected home, All collection, 2016 Carbine Expert Build PDP and empty cart through the rendered browser accessibility tree. Read-only only: no shopper actions, screenshot, viewport/mobile, Theme Editor, checkout or performance testing.

## Observations

- **Home:** video-led cycling hero, featured trail-bike shelf, product stories, category links, service propositions, promotional countdown, testimonial, blog and newsletter.
- **Collection:** 62 products, Size/Product Type/Price/Availability filters, alphabetical default sort, sport-category links and pagination. No controls activated.
- **Product:** $3,299 bike PDP with S/M/L/XL size radios, in-stock copy, purchase actions, specifications, service accordions and related/recent products. Some content references mixed model years.
- **Cart:** empty with Continue shopping and a Popular picks shelf.

Technical, fit, inventory, discount, warranty, testimonial and fulfillment statements are merchant claims and remain unverified.

## Coverage

- Selected homes: **66/127** (51 indexed HTML + 15 browser-rendered); 61 remain.
- Rendered pages: **60** across 15 selected presets.
- Indexed-document counts remain separate. Interaction, mobile, editor, checkout and Lighthouse counts remain zero.
